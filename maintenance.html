Computer maintenance

Good maintenance helps prevent common problems and can extend the useful life of your device. Start with these basic habits.

01

Keep it clean

Turn the device off before cleaning. Use a soft microfiber cloth, and keep liquids away from the computer.

02

Update software

Install operating system and application updates to receive improvements and important security fixes.

03

Protect your files

Back up important documents and photos to an external drive or a trusted cloud service.

04

Manage ventilation

Keep air vents clear and place the computer on a firm surface so heat can escape properly.

05

Stay safe online

Use strong, unique passwords and be careful with unexpected links, attachments, and downloads.

06

Shut down properly

Save your work and use the operating system's shutdown option instead of unplugging a running computer.

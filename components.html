Computer components

Computer hardware includes the physical parts you can touch. Each part has a role in making the computer work.

🧠

CPU (Processor)

Executes instructions and handles many of the computer's calculations. It is often called the computer's “brain.”

🎮

GPU (Graphics Card)

Processes images, video, and 3D graphics. Some computers use graphics built into the processor instead of a separate card.

🧩

RAM (Memory)

Temporarily holds the data and programs the computer is using. RAM is cleared when the computer is turned off.

💾

Storage (SSD or HDD)

Keeps files, applications, and the operating system even when the computer is powered off.

🔌

Motherboard and Power Supply

The motherboard connects components so they can communicate. The power supply converts electricity into power the PC can use.

🖱️

Peripheral devices

Keyboards, mice, monitors, webcams, and printers help people interact with a computer.

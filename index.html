What is a computer?

A computer is an electronic device that accepts data, processes it according to instructions, stores information, and produces results. Computers help us study, communicate, create, work, and play.

⌨️

Input

Enter data using devices such as a keyboard, mouse, microphone, or scanner.

⚙️

Processing

The processor follows instructions and works with data to complete tasks.

🖥️

Output

View or hear results through a monitor, speakers, printer, or other output device.
